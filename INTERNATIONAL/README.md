## 🎯 Why Ghost Protocol?

The security market is saturated with *Compliance* tools that generate endless lists of technical vulnerabilities but fail to answer the critical question: **"If we are attacked today, will our controls hold?"**

Ghost Protocol bridges the gap between theoretical compliance and operational reality.

### Strategic Differentiators
* **From Tool to Platform:** We do not just deliver error logs; we deliver **Decision Intelligence**.
* **Behavioral Validation:** Unlike static scanners, Ghost simulates **sophisticated adversary behavior**, testing your SOC's response capabilities and architectural resilience.
* **Realistic Scenarios:** Continuous assessment under operational stress conditions, modeling vectors that traditional automated tools ignore.

## 🏗️ Methodology & Architecture

Our approach utilizes a distributed architecture to model complex risks in heterogeneous environments. The system is designed to operate with low impact on business continuity, focusing on analytical precision.

<div align="center">
  <img src="../images/ghost_arch_layers.jpg" alt="Distributed Validation Architecture" width="100%">

  <sub><em>Fig 1. Modular Architecture: Segregation between telemetry collection, risk analysis, and executive presentation.</em></sub>
</div>

### 1. Assessment Layer (Input)
* **Function:** Evidence collection and surface telemetry.
* **Methodology:** Uses passive and active techniques to map real infrastructure exposure, simulating reconnaissance performed by external actors.

### 2. Analytical Layer (Processing)
* **Function:** Data Correlation and Threat Modeling.
* **Methodology:** Processes collected data to distinguish "technical noise" from "business risk," applying statistical models to predict compromise paths.

### 3. Decision Layer (Presentation)
* **Function:** Strategic Decision Support Interface.
* **Methodology:** Transforms technical data into prioritized decision artifacts, allowing managers to focus resources where risk is imminent.
